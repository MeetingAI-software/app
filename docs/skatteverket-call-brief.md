# Skatteverket call brief

Use this brief before any real Syncmemos payment is accepted. It prepares a factual conversation;
it is not legal or tax advice and a general phone answer may need written follow-up from a tax
professional.

## Contact and opening

Call Skatteupplysningen on `0771-567 567`, weekdays 08:00-16:00. Say:

> Jag behöver vägledning om hur två privatpersoner i Sverige ska hantera en planerad, återkommande
> försäljning av en webbaserad abonnemangstjänst. Betalningen ska gå genom Paddle, som marknadsför
> sig som Merchant of Record. Vi har ännu inte tagit emot riktiga betalningar och vill välja rätt
> säljare, skattehantering och företagsform innan vi gör det.

Describe only facts. Do not send identity documents, bank details, credentials, customer data, or a
home address by email or store them in this repository.

## Facts to have available privately

- both owners are Swedish residents and intend to share the project economically;
- the service is a recurring SaaS subscription sold online with a profit motive;
- intended prices are EUR 19/month for Solo and EUR 39/seat/month for Team, excluding tax;
- Paddle would collect the buyer's payment and remit a payout after fees;
- the first validation window would target three full-price customers;
- likely buyers include both Swedish consumers and businesses; and
- no company has been registered and no real payment has been accepted.

Correct any fact above before the call if the intended setup changes.

## Questions that require an answer

1. Does the planned recurring SaaS activity constitute business activity from the first sales,
   given its continuity and profit motive, or may the initial transactions be reported another way?
2. Must one or both owners register for F-tax, VAT, or another tax status before the first Paddle
   transaction?
3. How are Paddle payouts, Paddle fees, refunds, chargebacks, and foreign-currency differences
   recorded and declared by the selected supplier?
4. What is the VAT relationship between the Swedish supplier and Paddle as Merchant of Record,
   including any reverse-charge reporting or recapitulative statement?
5. Does the answer differ for Swedish consumers, Swedish businesses, EU customers, and customers
   outside the EU?
6. If one individual is Paddle's legal supplier while two people own the project economically, how
   must revenue, expenses, profit, and compensation between the owners be reported?
7. Is an individual supplier compatible with the planned shared ownership, or should the owners use
   a sole trader, simple partnership, trading partnership, or limited company before selling?
8. What records, vouchers, invoices, and Paddle reports must be retained, by whom, and for how long?
9. Does the small-business VAT exemption apply, and if so, how does it interact with Paddle's
   Merchant-of-Record model and cross-border services?
10. Which questions require a written ruling or advice from an accountant or lawyer rather than a
    general telephone answer?

Also ask the legal adviser reviewing the customer terms whether the online withdrawal function
introduced in Sweden on 19 June 2026 applies to Syncmemos subscriptions, what the function must do,
and how statutory withdrawal interacts with immediate access and the separate 14-day commercial
money-back guarantee.

## Evidence and decision record

Immediately after the call, create a private dated memo with:

- who called and the date/time;
- the facts presented;
- each answer, clearly separated from the owners' interpretation;
- links or forms the adviser referred to;
- questions that remained unanswered;
- required follow-up and owner; and
- whether written professional advice is still required before selecting the supplier.

Do not mark the repository's seller gate complete from memory or from an ambiguous answer. The
owners must sign a private decision that identifies one supplier and references the retained advice.
